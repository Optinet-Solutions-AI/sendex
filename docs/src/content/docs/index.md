title: Sendex
description: What Sendex is and where to go next.
---

Sendex is a bulk SMS marketing platform modelled on SMS Edge. You upload contact lists by country, build a campaign from those lists plus a sender ID group and a message text that carries a tracked link, and send it through SMS routes that Sendex rents from carriers (23Telecom over SMPP first). Sending starts with a small test batch per route so you can compare routes before committing the whole list, and can be scheduled in the campaign country's local time, paused, stopped or emergency-stopped at any point.

Every message is tracked from submission to outcome: sent, delivered and failed counts come back from the carriers as delivery receipts, clicks are captured by the short-link service at the edge, and registrations and sales arrive through postbacks from your landing pages. The results page turns those into CTR, cost per click, cost per lead and cost per sale so you can rank routes and campaigns by what they actually return. Start with [Upload a list](/using/upload-a-list/) if you are using Sendex, or [Architecture](/engineering/architecture/) if you are building it.
