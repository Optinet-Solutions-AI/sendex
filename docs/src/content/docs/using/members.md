title: Members
description: Invite people to your organisation by email, choose what they can do, change or remove their access.
---

Only people you invite can sign in to your organisation, and they see only your organisation. Nobody can sign up on their own or create an organisation of their own.

Open **Settings → Members**. Only a **co-admin** sees this page: the organisation's owner (for example Emily) and anyone she makes co-admin.

## Invite someone

1. Under **Invite someone**, type their email address and choose their **Access**.
2. Click **Send invitation**. They get an email with a link.
3. They open the link and sign in with **Google** (or with their email address). They land in your organisation.

Invitations waiting for an answer are listed under **Invitations waiting**; the **×** cancels one.

## Access levels

| Access | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Co-admin** | Everything you can do, including this Members page, settings, billing settings, brand deals and the emergency stop. |
| **Campaign manager** | Upload lists, create, send, pause and stop campaigns, see results. Not settings, members or billing. |
| **Viewer** | Look only: campaigns, lists, results and settings, without changing anything. |

## Change or remove access

- Change the **Access** next to a member: it applies the next time they load a page.
- The bin icon removes a member after you confirm. You can invite them again later.
- You cannot change or remove your own access, and the organisation always keeps at least one co-admin.

Every invitation and change is in **Settings → Logs** (`member.invite`, `member.role`, `member.remove`, `member.invite_revoke`).
