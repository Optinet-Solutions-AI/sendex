---
title: Schedule and stop
description: Send at a chosen local time, and pause, stop or emergency-stop a campaign that is running.
---

Sending can be started now or scheduled for later. Scheduled times are always in the **campaign country's timezone**, not yours, so a campaign for Italy set to 10:00 goes out at 10:00 in Rome whatever your own clock says. While a campaign runs you can pause it, stop it for good, or hit emergency stop to halt everything the organisation is sending.

## Schedule a send

1. On the campaign's **Send** page, instead of starting immediately choose **Schedule**. (coming on Day 2)
2. Pick the **date and time**. The timezone shown is the campaign country's; the equivalent in your own timezone is displayed next to it.
3. Choose what to schedule: a **test batch** on selected routes, or **Finish List** on a route you have already tested.
4. Confirm. The campaign shows as scheduled with a countdown, and the cost is reserved from your balance so the send cannot fail for lack of funds when the time comes.
5. To change your mind, open the scheduled send and **Cancel** it; the reservation is released.

## Pause, stop and emergency stop

- **Pause** holds the campaign where it is. Messages already handed to the carrier still go out, nothing new is submitted, and **Resume** carries on from the same point. Use it when you want to check something before sending more.
- **Stop** ends the campaign. The remaining numbers stay unsent and the campaign cannot be resumed; you would create a new campaign with the same lists to send to them later. Balance reserved for unsent messages is released.
- **Emergency stop** stops every running and scheduled campaign in the organisation at once and empties the send queue. Use it when a text is wrong, a link is broken or a route is misbehaving and you need everything to halt first and ask questions later. It is a single button on every campaign page and on the dashboard. (coming on Day 2)

## What "stopped" means at the carrier

Sendex can only stop what it has not yet submitted. A carrier may take a few seconds to minutes to deliver messages it already accepted, so the sent count can rise slightly after you stop. Delivery receipts for those messages are still recorded.
